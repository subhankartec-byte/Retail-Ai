01 Who we are

Retail AI ("Retail AI", "we", "us") provides browser-based tools that help retail store teams automate inventory analysis, stock validation, auditing, transfer planning and reporting. This policy applies to our website and the tools accessed through it. If you have questions, reach us at hello@retailai.in.

02 Information we collect

We keep collection to what the service actually needs:

Account information

  • Your name, email address and phone number, provided when you create an account.
  • Authentication data managed through our sign-in provider (for example, a securely stored password credential — we never see your password in plain text).

Business data you choose to upload

  • Retail files you load into the tools — POS exports, stock-on-hand (SOH) reports, item masters, sales and transfer data — so a tool can generate the report you asked for. How this is handled is described in section 03.

Usage & device information

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device and pages visited, used to keep the service working and improve it.
  • Local browser storage (localStorage) for your preferences and settings. This stays on your device.

03 Your uploaded store data

Most Retail AI tools run entirely inside your browser. When you upload an Excel or POS file to generate a report, that file is read and processed on your own device. The tools do not upload your raw store files to a Retail AI server, and we do not store copies of them.

Because the processing happens locally, closing or refreshing the page clears the loaded data from memory. If a future feature needs to send data to a server, we will make that clear before it happens and update this policy.

04 How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide, maintain and improve the tools and website.
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • To send essential service messages (for example, account or security notices). We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing you did not ask for.

05 The Ask AI feature

Some tools include an optional Ask AI assistant. It is off until you turn it on:

  • You add your own AI provider API key in the tool's settings. That key is stored only in your browser and is never sent to Retail AI.
  • When you ask a question, the on-screen report summary and your question are sent directly from your browser to the AI provider (Anthropic) to generate an answer.
  • If you do not add a key, the tool answers using built-in analysis and nothing leaves your device.

The AI provider processes that request under its own terms and privacy policy. Avoid including information in your questions that you would not want processed by a third-party AI service.

06 Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Retail AI, and only as needed:

  • Authentication & database (Google Firebase) — to sign you in and store your account profile.
  • Hosting (Vercel) — to serve the website and tools.
  • AI provider (Anthropic) — only for the optional Ask AI feature you enable, as described in section 05.

These providers may process data outside India under their own safeguards. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

07 Retention

We keep account information for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise your account information within a reasonable period, except where we must keep it to meet legal obligations. Uploaded store files are not retained by us (see section 03).

08 Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — encryption in transit, trusted infrastructure providers and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and to fix issues quickly.

09 Your rights & choices

You can:

  • Access or correct your account details from your profile, or by contacting us.
  • Request a copy of your personal information or ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features at any time.

To make a request, email hello@retailai.in. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

10 Children

Retail AI is a workplace tool intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will provide a clearer notice. Continuing to use Retail AI after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12 Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@retailai.in and we'll get back to you.
